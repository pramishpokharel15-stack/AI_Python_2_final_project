Game concept: The player is trapped inside a small square arena. The player controls a small circle and must dodge enemies, attacks, and projectiles to survive. Enemies enter the arena and attack the player. The objective is to survive all 10 waves and defeat the boss.

There are no towers or automatic defenses. The player is the only character being controlled.
1. Arena and Player
- The arena is a 3D-looking cube/square border occupying approximately 50% of the page's center.
- The player is a small circle inside the arena.
- The player can move in all directions inside the boundaries.
- Enemies spawn from the edges of the arena.
- Enemies attack the player using different attack patterns.
- Touching an enemy or its projectile causes damage.
- The player must dodge rather than automatically attack.
- The player cannot leave the arena.
